# Ratan Design v2 foundation approval

## Approval

The eight directional decisions in `ratan-design/plan.md` section 0.2 are approved for implementation through the `ratan-design-v2-parity` OpenSpec change.

Approval evidence: the repository owner explicitly invoked the OpenSpec apply workflow after reviewing and revising the final plan.
